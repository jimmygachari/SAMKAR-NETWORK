WHO WE ARE

About SAMKAR

Connecting People. Connecting Businesses.

SAMKAR Network Solutions is a technology and networking solutions company focused on providing reliable internet connectivity and professional network infrastructure.

We specialize in fibre optic installations, WiFi network deployment, network maintenance and customized connectivity solutions for homes, businesses and institutions.

Our goal is simple — to build reliable networks that allow our clients to work, communicate, learn and grow without unnecessary connectivity problems.
